❓ FAQ

Aksar puche jaane wale sawal

Kya offers real aur live hain?

Haan. Har search pe Swiggy ki public restaurant listing live fetch hoti hai — koi purana data nahi. Final discount checkout pe cart value ke hisaab se lagta hai.

Saare restaurants kaise aate hain?

Ek point se Swiggy sirf kuch restaurants dikhata hai, isliye hum aapki location ke charon taraf rings me (≈1 km se 4.5 km tak) multiple points scan karke sab combine karte hain. "Aur door tak scan" dabao toh aur restaurants aayenge.

"Max bachat" ka matlab kya hai?

Restaurant ke "cost for two" jitne order pe, offer ki limit (upto ₹X) ko dhyan me rakh ke andaza ki aap kitna bachaoge. Isi se "Best deal" ranking banti hai.

Location permission kyun?

Taaki exact aapke area ke offers dikhe. Location sirf aapke browser me rehti hai aur offers fetch karne ke liye use hoti hai. Permission na do toh city/area type kar sakte ho.
